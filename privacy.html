Privacy Policy

Shroffs Foundation Trust ("the Trust") collects and stores the following information through this donation receipt application: donor name, phone number, email address, postal address, PAN (where provided for 80G tax benefit), and donation details (amount, payment mode, purpose/head, date).

Why we collect this
To issue donation receipts, maintain the Trust's financial and accounting records, comply with Income Tax Act 80G requirements, and to send receipts to donors via WhatsApp or email where requested.

Who can access it
Only Trust staff with an approved clerk or admin login can view or enter donor information. Access is protected by individual logins; each staff account can be paused or removed by the Trust's admin at any time.

Where it's stored
Data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider, with access restricted to authenticated Trust staff. It is not sold or shared with third parties, except where required by law or tax authorities.

Data retention
Donation records are retained as required for the Trust's accounting and statutory tax-compliance purposes.

Contact
For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact info.anyapps@gmail.com.
